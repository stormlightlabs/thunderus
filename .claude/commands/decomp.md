---
description: Cut an idea or spec into issues and their sub-issues
argument-hint: [idea file, spec, or finding]
---

Use the `decompose` skill.

Source: $ARGUMENTS

File the issues a run can take and the sub-issues under them, writing through
`github-board`. At most five open sub-issues each; what does not fit is a
second issue. File an epic above them only when the work needs more than one
run, and put the blockers and file collisions that cross them in it.
Every sub-issue needs a stop rule checkable without judgment; if one cannot be
written, stop and say what decision is missing rather than filing it. Cite the
source document's identifier. Do not claim or start the work.
